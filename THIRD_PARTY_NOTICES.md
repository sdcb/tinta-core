Tinta Core includes md4c under `vendor/md4c`. See that directory's
`LICENSE.md` and `README.md` for its license and attribution. This is a
Tinta-specific fork with parser callback ABI 1, source ranges, parser-owned
entity/NULL decoding, native Tinta math delimiters, and structured Tinta HTML
events; it is not ABI-compatible with upstream md4c.
